# Platform capabilities, demand evidence, and integration access

Research date: 2026-10-22, America/Los_Angeles. Root workstream. Exa search result slots screened: 42 (8 searches; repeated URLs included). Two additional official-domain web searches were used to locate current OpenAI documentation. This is desk research, not an authenticated product trial or implemented integration.

## General-purpose platforms already cover much of the premise

- [Google AI Mode, August 27, 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/book-travel-ai-mode/): Google announced hotel booking in AI Mode with integrated hotel/OTA partners, beginning rollout in US English. The provider remains merchant of record and handles customer service. Flight search/price tracking is also supported, with airline/platform checkout for flights. This is stronger than the November 2025 announcement that described hotel checkout as a future capability. Rollout announcement does not verify availability for every account.
- [Gemini travel planning, August 6, 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/how-gemini-plans-trips/): Google's own account describes personalization from connected Google services, existing bookings in Gmail, Spark background itinerary organization, and Chrome-assisted flight form filling with final booking handed back to the user. These are vendor-described capabilities, not measured completion rates.
- [Gemini OpenTable help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17090712?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en): Supports finding, booking, cancelling, and reading reservations for eligible personal-account users in US/UK, English. The introduction says "change", but the explicit limitations say existing reservations cannot be modified directly; users receive a link. Use that specific limitation in comparisons. Payments, menus, and reviews are also outside this connector's documented operations.
- [Manus Browser Operator example](https://manus.im/blog/deep-dive-browser-operator-connector): Describes comparing Booking.com hotels with Google Maps walking distances, preparing the chosen reservation in a logged-in local browser, and pausing at credit-card completion. Cross-site browser operation is already offered. Marketing descriptions that imply local sessions eliminate CAPTCHA should not be treated as guarantees; [operator documentation](https://manus.im/docs/integrations/manus-browser-operator) explicitly allows manual intervention.
- [ChatGPT dots](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots): Current official documentation describes ongoing responsibilities, preferences and memory, background work, changing priorities, cloud browser access, and follow-up between conversations; rollout is gradual. Persistence, memory, and proactive follow-up alone are therefore weak novelty claims. This page does not establish a universally supported travel-booking service.
- [OpenAI WanderNote showcase](https://developers.openai.com/showcase/wandernote): Agent-assisted itinerary editing with schedule/map collaboration and WebMCP is an existing official example. A timeline, editable map, and WebMCP tool calls alone will not distinguish the proposed demo.

## Demand evidence and its limits

- [Booking.com Global AI Sentiment Report, July 23, 2025](https://news.booking.com/bookingcom-releases-the-global-ai-sentiment-report/): Online questionnaire in April–May 2025, 37,325 respondents across 33 markets. 89% expressed interest in AI for future travel planning; only 12% were comfortable with AI making decisions independently. The latter concerns AI broadly, not specifically permission to buy travel. This is a commissioned vendor survey and stated preference, not measured adoption or willingness to pay. Do not turn the difference into a funnel or market-size estimate.
- [Phocuswright public research preview, July 16, 2025](https://www.phocuswright.com/Travel-Research/Research-Updates/2025/From-hype-to-habit-78-percent-of-travelers-say-GenAI-improves-trip-planning): Across studied countries, one-quarter to one-third expressed interest in booking within a GenAI platform or letting an assistant book. The public preview does not expose enough methodology to treat this as a representative estimate for our intended segment. It is not inconsistent with the Booking.com result because the questions and samples differ.
- [Expedia/Luth 2023 research announcement](https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/eg-path-to-purchase-research/): An older seven-market study reported 141 pages of travel content and 303 minutes in the 45 days before booking. Survey sample: 5,713; behavioral panel: 70,000+, with limited mobile-app visibility. This supports historical fragmentation, not a universal 2026 average or proof that all research time is undesirable. Some travelers enjoy exploration.

Inference: investigate bounded delegation with visible outcomes. No reviewed study validates demand, paid conversion, or retention for a Tokyo arrival-night coordination product. Those require segment-specific interviews and observed tasks.

## Access constraints that change the demo

- [TableCheck API access](https://tablecheck.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/API): Access is reviewed case by case, components require approval, production credentials follow integration validation, and charges may vary. Public docs are not production access.
- [TableCheck Booking v1](https://tablecheck.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/API/pages/160334455/Booking+v1): Direct reservation writes need special arrangement; aggregator access requires individual venue approval. This component does not support payments or dining-experience menu bookings. Other components have different scope; do not claim TableCheck has no booking API.
- [Google Routes transit documentation](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes/transit-route): Supports departure or arrival times, fewer-transfer/less-walking preferences, and transit legs. Transit schedules can change. Routing data does not establish ticket availability, passenger eligibility, or a confirmed seat. A route integration remains proposed until account access and a real request work.

Implementation inference: build actual state-changing transport/hotel fixture services with independent authoritative records; use real official sources for rules and at least one genuine external read if accessible. Clearly label simulated inventory, external events, and payments. Production restaurant or transport writes must be separately qualified by supported access and tested outcomes.
